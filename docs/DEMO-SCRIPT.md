## Recording tips

- Record at phone width first (mobile-first design), then desktop.
- Keep sound off or use the in-app sound toggle; do not add music with
  unclear licensing.
- Do not show any real wallet, seed phrase, private key, or mainnet funds.
  This demo uses play tokens only.
